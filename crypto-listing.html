Can you guarantee exchange listing?+

No. Listing decisions are made independently by exchanges, and outcomes cannot be promised.

Do you provide legal advice?+

No. Compliance, token classification and licensing questions should be reviewed by qualified legal advisers in the relevant jurisdictions.

Can you manage outreach?+

Outreach and partnership support can be included with an approved scope. Claims, third-party fees and obligations are reviewed before engagement.
